Featured UX Case Study

Zepto Daily: Fixing Quick-Commerce Retention

10 user interviews
2 personas
4 problems prioritised
6-week A/B test

Zepto struggles to turn first-time users into daily habit users because the app gives them no reason to open it unless they urgently need groceries. I interviewed 10 users, built personas, prioritised four problems by user impact, business impact and feasibility, and designed Zepto Daily, a personalised daily deal feature with a morning notification, a Daily tab, a 'Your Usuals' reorder row and a streak reward. The case study also covers success metrics, a 6-week A/B test plan targeting D30 retention improving from 50% to 60%, risks and tradeoffs, and a phased launch plan.
